Setup in Minutes

From Download to Full Automation in 9 Steps

Getting started is simple. No technical expertise required.

1

Download & Install

Tap the download button to get the latest Bingwa Sokoni APK. Install it and allow installation from unknown sources when prompted.

2

Verify Your Number

Enter your phone number and confirm with the 6-digit OTP sent to you via SMS. Your account is secured end-to-end.

3

Insert Notification SIM

Make sure the SIM card linked to your M-Pesa Till or Paybill is active and receiving notifications in your phone.

4

Select Your SIM

In the app, select the Notification SIM slot — the one that receives the M-Pesa payment SMS messages.

5

Enable Accessibility

Grant the Bingwa Sokoni Accessibility Service permission. This is required for background USSD automation to work.

6

Ensure Airtime

Keep active airtime and SMS units on your SIM so the app can dial USSD codes and send customer confirmations.

7

Configure Data Plans

Go to Settings → Data Plans and configure your specific Safaricom Bingwa Sokoni offer codes and prices.

8

Customize Messages

Head to Notifications and write your own branded confirmation, failure, and marketing message templates.

✓

Save & Go! 🚀

Hit Save Changes and your automation is live. Bingwa Sokoni now handles every incoming payment automatically.
