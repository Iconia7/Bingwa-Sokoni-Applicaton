# Bingwa Sokoni Automation App 🚀

Automate your **Bingwa Sokoni** services for seamless and efficient management of your offers. This application streamlines the process of fulfilling digital service requests via M-Pesa notifications, ensuring immediate delivery and a superior customer experience.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Automated Fulfillment**: Instantly processes M-Pesa notifications to deliver data, SMS, and minutes.
- **Customizable Data Plans**: Easily edit and manage your offers to match your Safaricom Bingwa Sokoni rates.
- **Smart SMS Notifications**: Customize confirmation messages sent to customers after successful transactions.
- **Sim Selection**: Multi-sim support to choose your preferred notification and sending lines.
- **Airtime Management**: Real-time status checks to ensure you have sufficient balance for automation.

---

## 🛠️ Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up and start using the Bingwa Sokoni Automation App:

1.  **Download & Install**: 
    - Download the **Bingwa Sokoni.apk** file from this repository and install it on your Android device.
2.  **Notification Sim**:
    - Ensure the SIM card receiving M-Pesa payment notifications is active in the phone.
3.  **Sim Selection**:
    - Launch the app and select the **Notification SIM** (the one receiving the payments).
4.  **Initial Requirements**:
    - Ensure your SIM has active airtime and SMS units to send confirmation messages.

---

## ⚙️ Configuration Guide

### 1. Data Plans & Offers
Navigate to the **Settings Page** to configure your data plans. Ensure these match your specific Bingwa Sokoni offers for accurate automation.

### 2. Message Customization
Go to the **Notifications Page** to:
- Draft the professional messages sent to customers.
- Choose which SIM card will be used for sending the outgoing SMS.
- **Save Changes** to apply the new configuration.

---

## 💡 Best Practices

- **Keep the App Running**: For 24/7 automation, ensure the app is not optimized for battery saving by your OS.
- **Monitor Balance**: Regularly check your airtime and SMS balance to avoid service interruptions.
- **Test Your Setup**: Send a small test transaction to verify the automation flow and message delivery.
