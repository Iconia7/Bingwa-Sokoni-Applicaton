## 🛠️ Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up and start using the Bingwa Sokoni Automation App:

1.  **Download & Install**: 
    - Obtain the latest **Bingwa Sokoni.apk** from the [Releases](https://github.com/Iconia7/Bingwa-Sokoni-Application/releases) page.
2.  **Verify Number**:
    - Enter your phone number and verify using the 6-digit **OTP code** sent via SMS.
3.  **Notification Sim**:
    - Ensure the SIM card receiving M-Pesa payment notifications is active in the phone and selected as the **Notification SIM** in the app.
4.  **Initial Requirements**:
    - Ensure your SIM has active airtime and SMS units to send customer confirmation messages.
5.  **Enable Automation**:
    - For background automation to work, you MUST enable the **Accessibility Service** for Bingwa Sokoni in your phone settings when prompted.

## ⚙️ Configuration Guide

### 1. Data Plans & Offers
Navigate to **Settings > Data Plans** to configure your automation triggers. Ensure these match your specific Safaricom offers.

### 2. Manual USSD Trigger
Use the **Manual Trigger** page to execute specific USSD codes without waiting for an M-Pesa notification. This is useful for checking status or processing missed transactions.

### 3. Online Shop (Storefront)
Manage your public storefront link under **Settings > My Online Shop**. You can set your specific username which customers use to view your live offers online.

### 4. SMS Customization
Go to **Settings > Notifications** to:
- Draft professional "Success", "Failure", or "Already Active" messages.
- Use placeholders like `[first_name]`, `[phone]`, and `[offer]` for dynamic personalization.
- Choose which SIM card will be used for sending outgoing messages.

## 💡 Best Practices

- **Enable Accessibility**: Background USSD automation requires the **Bingwa Sokoni Accessibility Service** to be ON. The app will prompt you to enable this.
- **Battery Optimization**: Disable "Battery Optimization" for Bingwa Sokoni to prevent Android from killing the background fulfillment process.
- **Keep Airtime Ready**: High-volume stores should maintain a healthy airtime balance to avoid "Insufficient Funds" errors during peak hours.
- **Dual SIM Management**: Regularly verify that your "Notification SIM" and "Sending SIM" are correctly assigned in the header.
