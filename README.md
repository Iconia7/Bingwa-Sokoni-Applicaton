## 🛠️ Instructions on How To Use

Follow these steps to set up and start using the Bingwa Sokoni Automation App:

1.  **Download & Install**:  Obtain the latest **Bingwa Sokoni.apk** from the [Releases](https://github.com/Iconia7/Bingwa-Sokoni-Applicaton/releases/download/v2.0/Bingwa.v2.0.apk) page.
2.  **Verify Number**: Enter your phone number and verify using the 6-digit **OTP code** sent via SMS.
3.  **Notification Sim**: Ensure the SIM card receiving M-Pesa payment notifications is active in the phone.
4.  **Select Your Sim**: Launch the app and select the **Notification SIM** (the one receiving the payments).
5.  **Enable Automation**: For background automation to work, you MUST enable the **Accessibility Service** for Bingwa Sokoni in your phone settings when prompted.
6.  **Ensure Airtime**: Ensure your notification SIM has active airtime and SMS units to send confirmation messages.
7.  **Data Plans**: Go to the **Settings Page** and edit the data plans to match your specific Bingwa Sokoni offers.
8.  **Message Customization**: Go to the **Notifications Page** to customize the messages that will be sent to the customer and choose the SMS sending SIM.
9.  **Save Changes**: Click **Save Changes** and you're all done! 👌😊👏👏

## 💡 Best Practices

- **Keep the App Running**: For 24/7 automation, ensure the app is not optimized for battery saving by your OS.
- **Accessibility Service**: Background USSD automation requires the **Bingwa Sokoni Accessibility Service** to be ON.
- **Monitor Balance**: Regularly check your airtime and SMS balance to avoid service interruptions.
- **Test Your Setup**: Send a small test transaction to verify the automation flow and message delivery.
